winter2897 Vietnamese license plate dataset

Source: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1KLK-DWgT3VoQH4fcTxAt2eB3sm7DGWAf
(shared for training purposes by the dataset author; project:
https://github.com/winter2897/Real-time-Auto-License-Plate-Recognition-with-Jetson-Nano)

The upstream repository carries NO license file. This dataset is used for
TRAINING ONLY and is NOT redistributed: no dataset images or labels are
committed to this repository, and nothing derived from the dataset is
shipped except the detector weights trained on it (see LICENSES.md and
models/README.md - the detector was trained from scratch, so no
weight-derivative license applies).

The dataset contains images of cars and motorcycles with Vietnamese
plates; only the training-time annotation signal (bounding boxes of
plates) contributes to the shipped model.
